# Matching Protocol

## 1. Objective

Test whether the effective action of a two-component BEC can reproduce the relevant B4 operators under a fixed and explicitly stated field and parameter mapping.

A match of characteristic roots alone is insufficient.

## 2. Required inputs

Every matching calculation must specify:

- The B4 background (w, q, v, r).
- The B4 coefficients (p, s, kappa, Lambda).
- BEC masses (m1, m2).
- BEC densities (n1, n2).
- Interaction matrix G.
- Background velocities (V1, V2).
- The equation of state used: mean-field, LHY-corrected, or another explicitly stated model.
- Field normalization and reference frame.

## 3. Lambda_eff extraction

The derivation must identify:

1. The exact BEC operator associated with the candidate quartic spatial term.
2. The coefficient of that operator.
3. The definitions and dimensions of all A_i coefficients entering the derivation.
4. The field redefinition and canonical normalization.
5. The resulting Lambda_eff in the B4 convention.

The ratio Lambda_eff/Lambda_bare is meaningful only after both coefficients refer to the same normalized operator and dimensions.

## 4. Operator-level matching

Compare the complete fluctuation action, not isolated coefficients.

At minimum include:

- Quadratic terms.
- Cubic terms around the specified background.
- Quartic terms.
- Terms generated by field redefinitions.
- Frame transformations and their effect on the operator basis.

## 5. Fixed-parameter background test

After defining a parameter dictionary, hold microscopic couplings and EFT coefficients fixed while varying the backgrounds.

Do not independently refit the parameter dictionary for each background.

Record all failed and successful cases.

## 6. Spectral comparison

Characteristic-root agreement is a necessary diagnostic in some settings, but it is not sufficient evidence of operator equivalence.

Independent uncoupled components can reproduce arbitrary real quartets through pair grouping. Such a result must be classified as non-discriminating.

## 7. Numerical validity

For LHY-based calculations:

- State the perturbative order.
- Record the gas parameter and interaction regime.
- Monitor proximity to soft-mode or miscibility boundaries.
- Report numerical precision and convergence.
- Do not interpret a large higher derivative as controlled when the perturbative expansion has broken down.

## 8. Result classification

Each test must be classified as:

- PASS: the stated criterion is satisfied.
- FAIL: the stated criterion is violated.
- INCONCLUSIVE: the test cannot distinguish the models.
- NOT APPLICABLE: the test assumptions do not hold.

Every result must include assumptions, parameter values, units, and reproducible commands.
