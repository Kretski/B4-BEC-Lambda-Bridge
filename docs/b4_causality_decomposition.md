Reading: two independent sources. (i) the bare Λ>0 phi_x^4 stiffness makes the phi sound speed exceed 1 on any gradient q != 0, with no mediator; (ii) the Proca mediator is superluminal by itself (Λ = 0 still gives front > 1 everywhere sampled).
Consequences: no mediator alone restores the Minkowski cone while Λ>0 stays in the action; and the Proca no-go stands without Λ.
Limits: pointwise backgrounds; preferred-frame cone; 6 of 28 manifest backgrounds turn complex at Λ = 0 and are excluded from the Λ=0 statistics.
