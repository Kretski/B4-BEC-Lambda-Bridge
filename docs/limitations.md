# Limitations and Open Questions

## 1. No established BEC-to-B4 realization

The two-component BEC is currently a candidate analogue system. No complete microscopic or effective-action equivalence with B4 has been established.

## 2. Lambda_eff is not yet derived

The exact definitions of the coefficients A_i required for the proposed Lambda_eff extraction have not yet been supplied and independently verified.

Consequently, no numerical comparison between Lambda_eff and the B4 bare Lambda is currently claimed.

## 3. Spectral matching is non-discriminating

A previous local characteristic-root comparison found that 29 real B4 polynomials could be reproduced by a two-component hydrodynamic model, including the uncoupled limit.

This does not establish operator-level equivalence.

## 4. LHY perturbative limitations

Higher derivatives of the LHY equation of state can become large near soft-mode and miscibility boundaries.

A large derivative does not imply a controlled perturbative correction. The perturbative regime must be assessed independently for each parameter set.

## 5. Frame dependence

B4 is formulated with a preferred-frame structure, whereas the nonrelativistic BEC description is Galilean invariant.

An explicit physical frame mapping is required. Isolated frame-dependent operator coefficients cannot establish equivalence.

## 6. Field normalization

The comparison of coefficients requires an explicit field dictionary and canonical normalization.

Without these, numerical coefficient ratios can be meaningless.

## 7. Ultraviolet completion

The current BEC hydrodynamic model does not establish a UV completion of B4.

Finite-momentum Bogoliubov terms and microscopic BEC physics must be treated separately from the local low-energy EFT.

## 8. Open research questions

1. What are the exact definitions and dimensions of A_i?
2. Can a properly normalized Lambda_eff be derived?
3. Is its magnitude comparable to the B4 bare Lambda?
4. Can the complete cubic and quartic operator sectors be matched?
5. Can one fixed parameter dictionary reproduce a family of backgrounds?
6. Does any controlled BEC regime satisfy all required matching conditions?

## 9. Interpretation policy

A failed BEC matching test does not, by itself, falsify the broader hypothesis that Lambda may represent an effective correction generated by field interactions.

Likewise, a successful coefficient or spectral match does not establish that hypothesis without further independent tests.
