# UV acceptance protocol (B4 two-scalar EFT)

Criteria T1-T6 are copied verbatim from B4_CLOSURE_and_B5_PLAN.md (fixed before any scalar-mediator run). T7-T8 are additions
(reviewer, 2026-11-11), registered separately; they do not modify T1-T6.

T1 Reproduces p, s, kappa (rank one) in the low-energy limit (matching to stated order in k/M).
T2 Real spectrum and hyperbolic principal symbol (no Im(omega) ~ k) on the scanned backgrounds.
T3 Front velocity <= 1 (Minkowski cone) on the same 41 backgrounds + population cells (cuts 0.01-0.95). Report min/median of c_front.
T4 Perturbative with margin: g<=1 and rho<=0.3 (set A) as primary criterion; report g*rho = sqrt(X)-type invariants.
T5 Energy structure at fixed charges: h(k) positive (static criterion + Hamiltonian inertia); Krein signatures reported separately.
T6 If a preferred frame u^mu is needed: stated origin (dynamical or external) and its own causal/stability check.
T7 (added) Unique operator matching: the generated operator and the field normalisation are identified, not only spectral roots.
T8 (added) At least one measurable, stated observable, with sign and size of the correction.

Thresholds used for the scalar mediator run: T2 grid x=k/M in [1e-3,1e3], 121 points, rel. tol 1e-7; T3 front <= 1+1e-9;
T4 sqrtX_max <= 0.3; T5 min eigenvalue of lab-frame H2(k) >= -1e-12 on k/M in [1e-3,1e3].
Scope of each verdict: the named construction and the sampled backgrounds only.
