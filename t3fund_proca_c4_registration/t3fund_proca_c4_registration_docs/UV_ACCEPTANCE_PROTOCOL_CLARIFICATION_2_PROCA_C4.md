# Clarification 2 to UV_ACCEPTANCE_PROTOCOL_ADDENDUM_T3_T6.md: initial data of the Proca C4 (recorded before any C4 run)
Date: 2026-11-14. Decision of the project lead on the review of `t3fund_proca_c4_impl_bundle_20261114_v2.zip`. No physical criterion, threshold, background, resolution or outcome rule is changed.

1. Observation. P7 of the addendum (the general protocol) states "Gaussian wave packet(s) in the light fields ... heavy field unperturbed". Section 6 of `T3_FUND_PROCA_SPEC.md` (registered in commit e355af2, SPEC_HASH 86c360326da7406f, before any Proca evaluation) states "delta A1 Gaussian with delta F=0" together with delta f (right-moving) and delta g (left-moving), and a norm over delta f, delta g and delta A1.
2. Decision. For the Proca mediator the model-specific Section 6 applies as written: the C4 initial data contain the Gaussian delta A1 (delta F=0). This is an explicit exception to the "heavy field unperturbed" clause of P7 for this candidate. Reason: A1 is a dynamical field of the Proca model entering the registered norm and energy; exciting it at t=0 tests growth in all dynamical fields, not only in fields that receive amplitude through the coupling.
3. Not changed: the three designated backgrounds (`C4_designated_backgrounds.json`), the code (`t3fund_proca_c4.py`, sha256[:16] fd13ad278e32f713), M=1, the registered resolutions, T=1000, the norm criterion (<=3), the energy target (<=1e-9 calibrated on the free control), the 1% agreement, and the outcome rule as implemented in `aggregate_c4.py` (control misses target -> INCONCLUSIVE with priority over FAIL-UNSTABLE, per P7 and Section 6).
4. Scope of the conclusion. C4 is a time-domain test on the three designated constant-gradient backgrounds (two small-X PN, one PE with large gradients and front <= 2.0). It is not a statement about the stability of the whole population, of other backgrounds, or of the nonlinear evolution beyond these runs; it is not a T7 result and does not address B4.12 perturbativity.
5. If a variant with delta A1 = 0 is ever wanted as a diagnostic it requires a separate registered statement before it is run; none is registered here.
