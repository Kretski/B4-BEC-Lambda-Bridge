# T3-fund specification: Proca mediator coupled to two scalars (fixed-EFT, rank-one prescription)
Registered under UV_ACCEPTANCE_PROTOCOL_ADDENDUM_T3_T6.md (+ Clarification 1). All criteria, grids, tolerances and outcome classes of that addendum apply unchanged; this document only fixes the model, populations, closed forms, predictions and the C4 setup *before* any evaluation. Earlier Proca results (B4.10–B4.12, T3-B4 verdicts, perturbativity tables) are historical and are not carried over: C1–C4 are evaluated afresh for this model on new populations. Lessons from the SM+η Stage 1 are applied from the start: root/eigenvalue consistency checks are done in 50-digit arithmetic, not only in double.

## 1. Model and conventions (1+1D, metric (−,+), M=1 in the evaluations; the spectrum depends on x=k/M only)
Fields f, g (light scalars), A_μ=(A0,A1) (Proca, mass M). With F ≡ A1_t − A0_x and J ≡ g_φ f_t f_x + g_ψ g_t g_x:
 L = ½f_t² − ½f_x² − (Λ/4) f_x⁴ + ½g_t² − ½g_x² + ½F² + ½M²(A0² − A1²) + A1 J,  Λ ≥ 0.
Fixed-EFT rank-one prescription (as in B4.10): p=g_φ²/M², s=g_ψ²/M², κ=−g_φ g_ψ/M² = ∓√(ps), i.e. g_φ=M√p, g_ψ=−Mκ/√p (modes rank1_minus: κ=−√(ps); rank1_plus: κ=+√(ps)).
A0 is a Gauss-constraint field: (M²−∂_x²)A0 = −∂_x A1_t, i.e. A0 = −F_x/M² (no time derivative of A0 enters L).

## 2. C0 exact background
Constant jets f=wt+qx, g=vt+rx (w=f_t, q=f_x, v=g_t, r=g_x), A0=0, A1=Ā=(g_φ w q + g_ψ v r)/M² (=J̄/M²), F̄=0. All four Euler–Lagrange equations are satisfied identically (symbolic residuals 0, §8). C0 acceptance: EL residuals ≤1e-14 (double), ≤1e-40 (50 digits). Contact coefficients (M-independent): a_φ=Ā g_φ=p w q − κ v r, a_ψ=Ā g_ψ=−κ w q + s v r.

## 3. C1/C2 analytic
(i) Kinetic classification (time-derivative Hessian of the dynamical fields): κ_kin=(1 [f], 1 [g], 1 [A1]); the Gauss-constraint operator M²−∂_x² is positive definite. Priority rule of the SM+η spec §3(ii) (FAIL-GHOST for any κ<−τ; DEGENERATE for |κ|≤τ; τ=1e-12). The kinetic test is therefore passed by every background of the model; it is exercised by the control H3 (ψ kinetic sign flipped).
(ii) Exact dispersion identity (symbolic, verified §8): for plane waves e^{i(kx−ωt)}, c=ω/k, x=k/M, ε=x²/(1+x²):
 det K(ω=ck,k=Mx) = −M⁸x⁴(1+x²)·Q(c;ε),  Q(c;ε) = F(c) − ε c² d1(c) d2(c),
 d1 = c² − 2a_φ c − (1+3Λq²), d2 = c² − 2a_ψ c − 1, F = d1 d2 + p(w−qc)² d2 + s(v−rc)² d1.
 Q has degree 6 in c with leading coefficient −ε≠0 for every x>0; the six roots are the four scalar-like and two Proca-like branches.
(iii) Reduced first-order evolution system (A0 eliminated): with a=f_t, b=f_x, c̃=g_t, d=g_x:
 a_t=(1+3Λb²)b_x − g_φ[(F−F_xx/M²)b + A1_x a + 2A1 a_x],  b_t=a_x,
 c̃_t=d_x − g_ψ[(F−F_xx/M²)d + A1_x c̃ + 2A1 c̃_x],  d_t=c̃_x,
 A1_t = F − F_xx/M²,  F_t = J − M²A1.
 It reproduces the Euler–Lagrange equations (residual ≤3e-16 at random smooth states, §8) and conserves
 E = ∫dx{ ½a² + ½b² + (Λ/4)b⁴ + ½c̃² + ½d² + ½F² + F_x²/(2M²) + ½M²A1² }  (E ≥ 0 for Λ ≥ 0; relative dE/dt ≤2e-15 in a spectral test, §8).
 Linearised on the background, with scaled variables Ã=M δA1, F̃=(k̂/M)δF, k̂=√(M²+k²), the 6×6 matrix 𝓜(k) acting on (δa,δb,δc̃,δd,Ã,F̃) has only entries proportional to k or k̂ (no lower-order terms): 𝓜 = k P_s + k̂ P_k, with k̂/k=ε^{-1/2}. Its eigenvalues are −iω with ω the roots of Q·k (50-digit check ≤7e-48, §8). Hence the principal symbol at x→∞ is P_∞=P_s+P_k and its eigenvalues are k·(roots of Q(c;1)).
(iv) C1 (well-posedness w.r.t. the preferred time): if Q(c;1) has six simple real roots, P_∞ is diagonalizable with real eigenvalues, the remainder (k̂−k)P_k = O(1/k) is bounded, and the evolution is well posed in H^s for large k; for finite x the spectrum is real and simple iff the C3 criterion below holds, in which case γ(k)=0. C1 is evaluated per background as: strict hyperbolicity of P_∞ (six simple real roots of Q(c;1), tolerance 1e-9 relative) → analytic argument holds; repeated real roots → INCONCLUSIVE for C1; complex roots → FAIL-UNSTABLE (also detected by C3). C1 consistency checks (all in 50 digits): eigenvalues of 𝓜(k) match k·roots(Q) to relative 1e-9 at x∈{0.7, 3, 50}; at every C3 grid point γ(k) ≤ μ(k)(1+1e-9)+1e-9, where γ(k)=max Re eig 𝓜(k) and μ(k)=λ_max of the Hermitian part of V_∞⁻¹[𝓜(k)−kP_∞]V_∞ with V_∞ the eigenvector matrix of P_∞ (defined only when P_∞ has real simple eigenvalues; kP_∞ is skew-Hermitian in that basis, so γ(k)≤μ(k) by subadditivity of the logarithmic norm); everything is evaluated in 50-digit arithmetic (implementation logic: t3fund_proca_check_mu_mp.py). Scope of this check in the evaluation stage: all 41 PE cases and the first 100 PN draws of each of the 10 cells (1041 cases), at all 92 registered C3 grid points; V_∞ is recorded with its condition number and the minimal eigenvalue separation of P_∞; for the other PN cases only the C3 criterion is evaluated. Cases where P_∞ is not strictly hyperbolic are recorded as 'bound not defined' and are not counted as violations. Violation → INCONCLUSIVE (implementation inconsistency), not FAIL.
(v) C2 (principal symbol): at x=1e6 the speeds are real iff Q(c;1) has six real roots; the eigenvector matrix V_∞ of P_∞ has recorded condition number; no Jordan block iff the roots are simple.

## 5. C3 (mode stability) and registered predictions
C3 exactly as registered (k grid 61 points k/M∈[1e-3,1e3] + 31 in [1e3,1e6], classes with 1e-7/1e-5 thresholds, 50-digit confirmation r>1e-12, P2a k=0 check, P6 three-band rule with u(k)=|γ50−γ100|, margin 10, ≥3 consecutive refined points). The six roots at each k are computed from Q(c;ε(x)) built by exact polynomial arithmetic in arbitrary precision; for the 41 PE cases and for 100 PN draws per cell the same roots are also computed from the jet-Hessian determinant (Leibniz expansion, as in the SM+η Stage 1) at x∈{0.7,3,50} and compared to 1e-9.
k=0: det K(ω,0) ∝ ω⁴[M²(1+pq²+sr²)−ω²]·M²: four zero-frequency modes (removed before classification) and ω²=M²(1+pq²+sr²)>0; the k=0 class is real for every background.
Predictions (falsifiable; any counterexample is reported without changing a criterion):
 Pred-1 (identity): the numerical roots of det K from the jet Hessian equal the roots of Q to relative 1e-9 on all checked cases (PE and the PN subsamples above); the 𝓜(k) eigenvalues match to 1e-9 in the C1 consistency check.
 Pred-2 (k=0): the numerical k=0 class is real, with positive root ω²=M²(1+pq²+sr²) to 1e-9 relative, for every case.
 Pred-3 (UV speeds): at k/M=1e6, for cases whose six roots are real, the sorted speeds equal the roots of Q(c;1) within 1e-4.
 Pred-4 (exact criterion): for PE and the first 100 draws of every PN cell, let E = "six real roots of Q at ε=1e-4 and the discriminant of Q with respect to c has no real root in (0,1]" (exact rational arithmetic on the binary values). Then E ⇒ C3 PASS and C3 FAIL ⇒ not E. Either counterexample is reported. (The converse "C3 PASS ⇒ E" is not predicted: complex bands between two discriminant roots are possible.)
 Pred-5 (ghost): H3 40/40 FAIL-GHOST; every PE/PN case has κ_kin=(1,1,1) (no FAIL-GHOST, no DEGENERATE).
 Pred-6 (IR): if the four-root polynomial F(c) has a root with |Im c|>1e-3 (complex local EFT roots), the first grid point k/M=1e-3 is complex and the case is C3 FAIL-UNSTABLE.
Diagnostic (not part of any outcome): the UV front speed max|c| of Q(c;1); superluminal fronts are allowed under preferred-foliation causality. Historical context stated before registration (B4.11, other seed): principal-symbol fronts were >1 in all sampled cells and complex principal symbols appeared only in the cut-0.95 cells (≈4–8%); the C3 outcome of the PN populations is not predicted by this document.

## 6. C4 (time domain)
Designated backgrounds (rule fixed before any evaluation, independent of outcomes): (a) the first two PN draws in manifest order (cell order cut 0.01 → 0.95, rank1_minus before rank1_plus) that are C3 PASS and have UV front speed max|c| ≤ 2.0; (b) the first PE case in manifest order that is C3 PASS with max|c| ≤ 2.0. The bound max|c|≤2.0 is a numerical-stability rule for the registered resolutions: with pseudo-spectral k_max=π/dx=50.3 at (16384, dt=0.025) the RK4 condition ω_max·dt<2.83 gives max|c|<2.25. A designated background that is not C3 PASS is NOT-RUN with no replacement. If a rule selects nothing, C4 is NOT-AVAILABLE for that rule (reported; e.g. the B3 anchor, whose front exceeds 2, is outside the C4 domain).
Method as registered C4: periodic box 1024, pseudo-spectral D, RK4, T=1000, records every 5, base resolution (16384, dt 0.025), convergence (32768, 0.0125). State (a,b,c̃,d,A1,F) evolved with the reduced system of §3(iii) (Fourier multipliers for F_xx); the implementation is checked against the independent sympy Euler–Lagrange operator at random states (≤1e-12) before any run. Energy E of §3(iii) (full nonlinear, conserved), E_fl = E − E_bg.
Perturbation: δf right-moving (δf_t=−Dδf), δg left-moving (δg_t=+Dδg), δA1 Gaussian with δF=0; Gaussian s=15, k0p=0.5, centre 0, amplitude ε=1e-3·max(|q|,|r|). Norm 𝒩=√(dx Σ(δf²+δg²+δA1²)); criterion max 𝒩/𝒩(0) ≤ 3. Free control: g_φ=g_ψ=0 (Ā=0), same gradients, Λ, perturbation, resolutions. Outcome rule as registered C4 incl. Clarification 1 (PASS: ratio ≤3 at both resolutions, total energy drift ≤1e-9 reached, control reaches target, 1% agreement; INCONCLUSIVE: control misses target / 1% agreement fails / drift missed with control passing / non-finite values; FAIL-UNSTABLE: ratio >3 with agreement; never FAIL on energy alone). Stated limitation: total-energy drift is dominated by the background; the norm criterion is substantive. Passing C4 on small-X backgrounds is weak evidence for the strong-X ones.

## 7. T7 and perturbativity — not part of this document
T7 for the Proca mediator (integrating out A at tree level and matching to the B4 operators) is a separate stage to be registered in its own document before it is run, and only if at least one population reaches C3/C4 PASS. For orientation only: at leading order in 1/M², A1≈J/M², L_eff ⊃ J²/(2M²)=½[p(f_tf_x)² − 2κ(f_tf_x)(g_tg_x) + s(g_tg_x)²] with the B4.10 definitions of p,s,κ; whether this equals the B4 operator structure including normalisations and higher-order terms is not assumed. The perturbativity criterion of the original protocol (B4.12: control sets A,B 0/41, set C only at α≈1) is not evaluated here; a C3/C4 PASS would not resolve it, and nothing here constitutes a UV completion of B4.
